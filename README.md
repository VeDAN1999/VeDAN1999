## Research

- **Complex paths for real stochastic processes** — D. A. Baldwin, A. J. McKane, S. P. Fitzgerald (2026). [arXiv:2604.09398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09398)
  Revisits the path-integral calculation of metastable decay rates and shows that a complex extremal solution arising in the Itô formulation removes a step previous derivations could not justify.
- **PhD thesis: *Complex saddles in stochastic path integrals*** — University of Leeds, 2025. [White Rose eTheses](https://etheses.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/38065/)
  Picard–Lefschetz theory as a rigorous framework for weak-noise stochastic path integrals; introduces the *stochastic complex bounce* and computes escape rates for the cubic and sine-Gordon potentials.
